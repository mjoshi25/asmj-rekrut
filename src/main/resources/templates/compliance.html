TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY

Compliance & policies

Our principles for responsible recruitment and handling candidate information.

Privacy and personal information

ASMJ Recruitment uses account and application information to provide recruitment services.

Joining documents and offer letters are stored separately from public pages and are served through authenticated access checks. Users should not upload information unrelated to the recruitment process.

Recruitment fairness

Employers and recruiters are expected to use candidate information only for legitimate recruitment purposes, apply fair and job-related selection criteria, and avoid discriminatory practices. Candidates should provide accurate information and only submit documents they are authorized to share.

Data security and retention

Access controls, private file storage and activity records help protect recruitment records. Authorized administrators should establish retention and deletion schedules, respond to data-access requests and manage any suspected security incident under the applicable policies and laws.

Do not share login credentials or publicly distribute candidate documents. Report suspected unauthorized access to the ASMJ platform administrator.

Terms of use

Users are responsible for the accuracy and lawful submission of information they provide.

Policy status

These sections are a starter policy summary, not a substitute for legal advice. ASMJ should review and publish formal Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, retention periods, grievance contact details and applicable legal disclosures before relying on them as final legal terms.
